# Surface brief: portfolio home (index.html)

Scope: the whole one-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience (the studio leads), with a Persuade job (start a conversation).
Audience: prospective client or collaborator first, recruiter second. Action: mail Sila. Proof: selected work, experience rows, the live studio. Constraints: English primary with Thai where it earns trust; coral accents only; content readable without WebGL; placeholders labelled once in the footer, never per entry.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A premium-minimal gallery page with one object, the studio, laid out like the supplied reference (monogram, caps nav, name-and-roles column, graphic on the right); it refuses the hero-plus-cards portfolio and the dense mono résumé.
OWN-WORLD: ground #f6f5f2, ink #141311, muted #6f6b66, hairline #e2dfda, coral #ce4257 as one dot, on hotspot rings and hover. Archivo condensed 800 for monogram, name and email; Geist for everything else; 11px tracked caps labels; hairline rows; no cards, shadows or radii.
STORY: the visitor sees the room and the name, reads three roles and one sentence, and clicks Start a conversation.
FIRST VIEWPORT: full-viewport 5/7 split. Header row: monogram left, tick strip centre, caps nav right. Left column centred: location, name, three roles, lede, coral-dot link. Right: square studio, max 70svh, three coral ring hotspots. Footer row: roles left, links right. Mobile stacks header, left column, room.
FORM: Gallery, reference layout, light ground; chosen over dark. Signature interaction: hotspot click moves the studio camera to the object, then the page scrolls to the section.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
