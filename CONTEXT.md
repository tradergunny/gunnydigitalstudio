# Context: GunnyTrader portfolio

Glossary of terms used when talking about the portfolio. Definitions only, no implementation.

## Studio
The 3D model of Gunny's personal workroom, built in Blender. On the site it is the hero stage: a scene shown beside the headline that a visitor can look around. The studio render is the still image of the same room used whenever the live scene is not available.

## Hotspot
A labelled marker on the studio anchored to one object. There are three: the chart monitor (Trading facet), the laptop (Work), the camera (Content facet). Activating a hotspot focuses the studio camera on that object and then takes the visitor to the matching section.

## Section
One of the five top-level parts of the page, in order: Intro, Work, Experience, About, Contact.

## Facet
One of the three sides of Gunny's identity presented inside the About section: Developer, Trader, Creator. Trader and Creator link out to external channels; Developer links to Work.

## Primary action
Starting a conversation (contact). The one thing the page is designed to get a visitor to do.

## Primary visitor
A prospective client or collaborator for full-stack work. The secondary visitor is a recruiter or hiring manager scanning experience.

## Work
Selected software projects Gunny built. Trading is discretionary and is not Work.

## GunnyTrader
The public brand and wordmark, used everywhere on the site. Sila is the personal name behind it, introduced once in the Intro.

## Placeholder
A Work entry, experience row, testimonial or channel link that is synthetic and visibly labelled as such, present only until Sila replaces it with real material.

## Monogram
The stacked three-line wordmark (GU / NN / Y) in the top-left corner of every page. It is the only place the name appears abbreviated.

## Hero
The first full-viewport screen: header row, the name column on the left, the studio on the right, footer row. Everything a visitor needs to act is inside it.

## Poster
The still WebP render of the studio that paints first on every device and remains when the live scene cannot load. Replaces the term "studio render" above.

## Live scene
The studio rendered in real time in the browser, loaded after the poster and faded in over it.
